Purpose

What the stream wants to achieve

Purpose

We want viewers to learn about our everyday student life, routines, and real-life experiences — the small moments, hardships, struggles, challenges, unpredictable moments/experiences, and joys that we go through daily. We wish and hope our viewers will understand that every ordinary day is meaningful, exciting, see the effort behind our daily tasks, and feel inspired to appreciate and share their own stories and comments too.

Message

Ordinary days can still be inspiring, exciting, and worth sharing for the viewers to relate to and learn from.

Goal

To help viewers understand real-life routines and feel connected to the story whenever we can share about our daily experiences.

Audience

Who the stream is for

Target viewers

Our planned audience are mainly students like us, teenagers, youths, and adults who share the same interests as us. We want to create an enjoyable, entertaining, safe, and positive environment for our viewers.

Audience need

The content that we need for our vlog needs to be entertaining and easy to understand and meaningful too, since our target is young Filipino students who use phones a lot of time, our vlog aims to provide useful and educational information in an engaging way.

Topics

Main ideas to discuss

Daily routine

Having an “unfiltered storytime” where we can pick a specific, entertaining event that happened recently. As it can work as it portrays that sharing personal stories makes the audience feel like they are part of the story which is engaging.

Storytime

Answering “deep” questions from the audience, since our chosen genre is about vlogging. When you discuss questions that the viewers submitted beforehand to you it creates knowledge that the viewers can think about and let you know who you are.

Audience Q&A

Our viewers will be doing challenges that they do and they can also share it in the chat. They will also do a suggestion box to keep them interested and waiting for what they want us to do.

Programming Guide

Comparison, Logical Operators, and Switch

Comparison Operators

Used to compare values.

let score = 10;
console.log(score == 10); // true
console.log(score > 5); // true
  • == equal to
  • != not equal to
  • > greater than
  • < less than

Logical Operators

Used to combine conditions.

let age = 18;
if (age >= 18 && age < 60) {
  console.log("Adult");
}
  • && AND
  • || OR
  • ! NOT

Switch Statement

Used when checking multiple fixed values.

let day = 2;

switch (day) {
  case 1: console.log("Monday"); break;
  case 2: console.log("Tuesday"); break;
  default: console.log("Other day");
}
  • case value checks equality
  • break stops the check
  • default handles the rest

Group information and stream plan

Group information

Stream idea

Each member will record a short part of their ordinary day, including morning routines, school preparation, hobbies, and responsibilities. The goal is to make the content relatable and entertaining.

Purpose

We want viewers to learn about everyday student life, struggles, joys, and the effort behind ordinary activities. The stream also aims to inspire people to appreciate their own daily experiences and share their stories.

Audience

Our target audience is students, teenagers, young adults, and other viewers who enjoy relatable vlogs and positive content. We want to create a safe and engaging environment for them.

Main topics

  • Unfiltered storytime from recent events.
  • Answering audience questions and discussing meaningful experiences.
  • Daily routine and realistic student life.

Activities

Viewers may join challenges, send reactions in the chat, and share suggestions through a suggestion box to keep the stream interactive and engaging.

Expected viewers and length

We expect around 500 to 1000 viewers, depending on engagement and content quality. The stream will last around 9 minutes, with each member contributing about 1 minute and 30 seconds.

SMART goal summary

By September 18, our group will prepare a vlog-style stream for students and young viewers. We will include 2 to 3 main topics, activities, and questions in a 9-minute stream to make it engaging, relatable, and easy to understand.
